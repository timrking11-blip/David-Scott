# Evidence ledger

Sub-project 1, evidence and feasibility research. Client: David Scott Company (stakeholder: Brad). Advisor: Timothy King. Ledger date: 2026-11-04. Status: draft, merged from the five reviewed research domains. Schema, strength scale and rules follow spec section 6 of `docs/specs/2026-11-04-evidence-feasibility-design.md`.

Later deliverables cite IDs from this file only. Rows are sorted by ID.

## Strength scale (spec section 6)

- S1: official vendor documentation, terms or pricing page that names this product line and version.
- S2: official vendor source, but general (not version-specific) or marketing.
- S3: authorized partner, marketplace listing, or reputable independent source.
- S4: forum post, undated page, or analyst inference.
- SR: stakeholder-reported. Not ranked on the scale; always needs validation.

## Conventions

- `verified` means the cited source states the claim, confirmed by fetching it. It does not mean tested. No row is PoC-validated; that status is reserved for later tested results.
- `hypothesis` means inference, or the source was read only through a search snippet ("snippet only").
- A snippet-only row keeps its source grade, but it counts as S4 for path support.
- A `verified` row may carry "quoted, unverified" on a price. That means the source states the price, but it is not confirmed for Brad.
- The Accessed date is the fetch date.
- Assumption A-01: Brad runs US Sage 50 Accounting; edition and version unknown.
- Assumption A-02: "GrokBot super" means SuperGrok (xAI consumer plan); stakeholder intent unconfirmed.
- Competing reading A-02b: "GrokBot super" means Grok Bot on the X Subscription Super plan. Rows testing this reading are marked in the Claim column (for example "A-02b alternative").
- Stakeholder-reported rows have Source type `stakeholder`, Strength `SR` and Status `stakeholder-reported`; they come from the master prompt as relayed by Timothy and always need validation. The tl;dv recording was not reviewed and is never cited.
- SR rows use the Source string "Master prompt business context (stakeholder-reported via Timothy)".
- The Accessed date on SR rows is the date the brief was received (2026-11-04), not a fetch date.
- A conflict is recorded in the Conflicts with column of both rows. The stronger source wins only when both cover the same product, version and region; otherwise both rows carry UNRESOLVED-CONFLICT in Limitations and any matrix cell citing either row says Unresolved (spec section 6, Rule 4).
- Where no method was found, the wording is "No supported method found in sources checked", with the sources listed in the Sources checked section at the end of this file.
